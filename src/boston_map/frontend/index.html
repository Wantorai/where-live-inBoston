    How to read this map

    Density = estimated population ÷ land area. Full tract boundaries can include water, but water is excluded from the calculation. Gray means no density value; it is different from zero. Ocean tract 990101 (zero population and land area) is hidden from the map and tract selector, but retained in the downloadable dataset.

    White alone includes people reporting only White, regardless of Hispanic/Latino origin. Everyone else includes people reporting multiple races. Demographics describe the tract population, not neighborhood quality.

    Published margins of error refer to population counts (90% confidence). Margins for density, percentages, and the derived “everyone else” count have not been calculated. These are five-year estimates, not current counts.

    Sources: U.S. Census ACS and TIGER/Line. Boston neighborhood outlines will be added separately.
